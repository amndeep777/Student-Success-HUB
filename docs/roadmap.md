# MVP roadmap

1. Discovery and governance: 1 week (MVP)
2. Architecture and database: 1 week (MVP)
3. Core API and privacy middleware: 3 weeks (MVP)
4. Web app: 2 weeks (MVP)
5. Video interviews: 3 weeks (MVP, one-to-one)
6. Notes: 2 weeks (MVP)
7. Exams: 4 weeks (MVP, two subjects)
8. Hardening and pilot: 4 weeks (MVP)
9. Mobile: later, optional
10. Explainable ML: later, optional
11. Panel interviews and additional calendar providers: later, optional

Pilot go-live requires governance sign-off from the Data Protection/Privacy Officer, Dean or HoD, Student Welfare/Counselling Head, and student representative.
